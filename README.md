# Hi, I'm Skye Xi

I study Data Science and Business & Finance at NYU Shanghai. My projects explore how to evaluate quantitative strategies and make complex datasets easier to explore.

## Selected projects

- **[RRG Sector and ETF Rotation](https://github.com/skyexry/rrg-etf-rotation)** — Compared four ETF rotation and benchmark strategies under shared execution and transaction-cost assumptions, and tested how the results changed with costs and parameters. [Research report](https://github.com/skyexry/rrg-etf-rotation/blob/main/reports/english/RRG_Report.pdf)
- **[Hit Atlas](https://github.com/skyexry/hit-atlas)** — An interactive D3.js team project that explores the lyric emotions, word use, artist origins, and sound of Billboard Year-End Hot 100 hits across four chapters. [Interactive demo](https://skyexry.github.io/hit-atlas/atlas/)
